Levinorises slowly

A small bar built around one idea: the best cup takes the time it takes. We roast in small batches, pour one cup at a time, and let the crema tell you when it's ready.

The name

Where "levare"
meets the cup.

"Levino comes from levare — to lift, to rise. It's what a good crema does. It's what we hope your morning does too."

We started Levino because most coffee bars had gotten faster, but not better. So we slowed one thing down: the pour. Every espresso rests a beat longer than you'd expect, every filter cup is timed to the second, and every bag of beans is roasted in batches small enough that we taste each one before it goes on the bar.

The result isn't complicated. It's just coffee, given the time it asks for. Come sit at the counter and watch it happen — that's the whole design of the place.
